# Demo script (five minutes)

1. **Overview** (30 s). Five tiles, five stories. "Two of these are the point: an incident and a weak signal."
2. **Adoption** (60 s). Executive Sales Scorecard at the top with 271 viewers a week. Platform viewers are distinct
   accounts: show that the weekly total is far less than the sum of report viewers. Point at *What excluding service
   accounts changes*: one report loses 69% of its views under v2.
3. **Reliability** (90 s). SLA priority: five reports share the same 90.0% compliance but the scorecard ranks first on
   usage. October timeline: bars of hours stale, views while stale. Next chart: the largest retailer's SFTP file late on
   the same nine mornings. "The fix is upstream; the priority comes from usage."
4. **App feedback** (60 s). Android 5.2.0 at 2.57 stars on 281 reviews, the interval clear of zero, pairing at 52% of
   reviews. 5.2.1 recovers. "This is actionable."
5. **Product ratings** (45 s). Arc Speaker Mini in Japan: 1.67 stars, but on three reviews. Shrunk, it is 3.58. "Same
   kind of drop, different evidence: watch it."
6. **Data checks** (30 s). The API schema change: a naive filter goes to zero on 15 September. The rules table: each rule
   names the test that enforces it.

Close: the incident review in `analysis/incident_review.md` is regenerated by CI from the same marts.
